## 📱 App Store & Play Store Submission Requirements

To successfully submit the application to **Apple App Store** and **Google Play Store**, the following assets and information are required.

Most of these are **mandatory** and are reviewed by Apple and Google.

---

### 🎨 App Branding Assets

- [ ]  **App Icon**
    
    High‑resolution app icon (square, no transparency, no rounded corners).
    
- [ ]  **Splash Screen / Launch Screen**
    
    Logo or visual shown when the app is opening.
    
- [ ]  **App Name & Subtitle**
    
    Official app name and (optional) short tagline.
    

---

### 📝 Store Listing Content

- [ ]  **App Description (Short & Full)**
    
    Marketing description explaining the app’s purpose and main features.
    
- [ ]  **Screenshots (Required)**
    
    Screenshots of key app screens for:
    
    - iPhone & iPad (App Store)
    - Android phones (Play Store)
- [ ]  **App Category**
    
    Primary and secondary category selection.
    
- [ ]  **Keywords (App Store)**
    
    Search keywords to improve discoverability.
    

---

### 🔐 Legal & Compliance

- [ ]  **Privacy Policy URL**
    
    Public URL explaining how user data is collected and used (mandatory).
    
- [ ]  **Terms & Conditions URL**
    
    Public URL for app terms (recommended / sometimes required).
    
- [ ]  **Data Safety Information (Play Store)**
    
    Declaration of data usage, storage, and sharing.
    

---

### 🧪 Review & Testing

- [ ]  **Test Account Credentials**
    
    Demo account (email & password) for Apple/Google reviewers to access the app.
    
- [ ]  **Special Review Notes**
    
    Any explanation needed for reviewers (e.g. login flow, permissions, restricted features).
    

---

### 🌍 Localization (If Applicable)

- [x]  **Supported Languages**
    
    Confirmation of available languages (e.g. EN, FR).
    
    ✅ We need Kaptrain to be available in FR and EN
    
- [ ]  **Translated Store Content**
    
    App name, description, and screenshots per language (if required).
    

This will help us ensure a smooth setup, testing, and production release.

---

### ⏳ Review Timeline Notes

- App review usually takes **several days** (sometimes longer during holidays).
- Apple and Google may **request changes or clarifications** before approval.
- Resubmission may be required if feedback is provided.

---

### ℹ️ Notes

- We will guide and prepare the technical submission.
- All branding, legal text, and marketing content must be provided or approved by the client.

This ensures a smooth submission and faster approval process.
